Hue & Seek — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Hue & Seek is a multiplayer hide-and-seek game for iOS. This policy explains exactly what the app handles. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

What the app collects

DataWhyWhere it goes
Anonymous player ID Required to join a multiplayer room and to keep a match session alive. Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby, Relay). Not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID.
IP address Technically required to route match traffic between players. Processed transiently by Unity Relay to establish the connection. Not stored by us.
Game progress (rounds played, wins, best camouflage score) To show your own statistics. Stored only on your device. If you enable Game Center, your score may be submitted to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center nickname.

What the app does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics or ad SDKs.
  • No in-app chat, so no message content is processed.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no account creation or personal data entry exists, no child-specific data is gathered.

Deleting your data

On-device statistics are removed when you delete the app. The anonymous player ID is managed by Unity Gaming Services; to request its deletion, contact us at the address below and we will forward the request. Game Center scores are managed by Apple and can be removed from your Game Center account settings.

Third-party services

The app uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby and Relay) to connect players, and optionally Apple Game Center for leaderboards. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies:

  • Unity privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Apple privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date at the top.

Contact

For privacy questions or to request deletion of your data, contact us via samofy.co.uk.
