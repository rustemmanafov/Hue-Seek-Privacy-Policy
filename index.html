Hue & Seek — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Hue & Seek is a multiplayer hide-and-seek game for iOS and Android. This policy explains exactly what the app handles. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

What the app collects

DataWhyWhere it goes
Anonymous player ID Required to join a multiplayer room and to keep a match session alive. Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby, Relay). Not linked to your name, email, Apple ID, or Google account.
IP address Technically required to route match traffic between players. Processed by Unity Relay and Lobby to establish the connection; they may derive an approximate region from it to pick the nearest server, and keep technical diagnostics needed to run the service. Not stored by us.
Game progress (rounds played, wins, best camouflage score) To show your own statistics. Stored only on your device. On iOS, if you enable Game Center, your score may be submitted to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center nickname. On Android, statistics never leave the device.

Clip capture (iOS only, off by default)

The app can record the last few seconds of your own screen so you can share a moment from a match. This is off until you turn it on in the main menu, and iOS shows its own permission prompt the first time it runs. The Android version has no clip capture.

  • Recording uses Apple's ReplayKit and only runs during the search phase of a match. The microphone and camera are never enabled.
  • Footage stays in a temporary buffer on your device. Nothing is written to a file, and nothing leaves your device, unless you tap "Share".
  • When you tap "Share", iOS's own share sheet opens and you choose where the video goes. We never receive it and never upload it.
  • Turning the setting off stops the buffer immediately.

What the app does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • No contacts, microphone, or camera access.
  • The app never reads your photo library. If you pick "Save Video" in the share sheet, iOS asks your permission once to add that clip to Photos — nothing else is accessed.
  • No screen recordings are ever sent to us — see "Clip capture" above.
  • No precise (GPS) location. The only location-related data is the approximate region described under "IP address" above.
  • On Android the app requests only two permissions: Internet access (to play online) and vibration (haptic feedback). Sharing a room code opens Android's own share menu; the app sees nothing about where you send it.
  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics or ad SDKs.
  • No in-app chat, so no message content is processed.

Children

Hue & Seek is made for players of all ages, including children. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat, and never asks for a name, email address, photo, or any other personal detail. It does not use the advertising ID or any hardware identifier (such as IMEI or MAC address), and it never requests location permission.

To run online matches, the same limited data described above is processed for every player, including children: the anonymous player ID and the approximate region Unity Relay and Lobby derive from the IP address to pick the nearest server. This data is used only to make multiplayer work — never for advertising, profiling, or tracking. Players cannot send messages to each other; other players see only your painted character and your in-game actions.

A parent or guardian can ask us to delete the anonymous player ID at any time (see "Contact" below).

Deleting your data (Hue & Seek)

In the app (instant):

  1. Open Hue & Seek and stay on the main menu.
  2. Tap Delete My Data, then tap it again within 4 seconds to confirm.

This permanently deletes your anonymous player ID from Unity Gaming Services (Authentication) and clears your on-device statistics (rounds played, wins, best camouflage score). An internet connection is needed for the first part. Your next online match simply starts with a brand-new anonymous ID.

By email: write to support@samofy.co.uk with the subject "Hue & Seek data deletion". Because the player ID is anonymous we cannot look it up for you, so the in-app button is the reliable way; we will help if it does not work for you.

What is kept: we keep nothing. Technical connection logs that Unity Relay and Lobby create while a match is running (including the approximate region) are held by Unity under its own retention policy and are not linked to you by us. Uninstalling the app also removes all on-device data. On iOS, Game Center scores are managed by Apple and can be removed in your Game Center account settings.

Third-party services

The app uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby and Relay) to connect players, and on iOS optionally Apple Game Center for leaderboards. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies:

  • Unity privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Apple privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy
